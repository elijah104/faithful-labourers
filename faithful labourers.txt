# Faithful Labourers
> **Rooted in God. Serving Communities. Transforming Lives.**
Faithful Labourers is a Christian organization focused on spreading the Gospel, supporting communities, establishing churches, and helping people in need.
## About Us

We are committed to:

* Spreading the Gospel of Jesus Christ
* Establishing and supporting Pentecostal churches
* Helping children, families, and elderly people
* Supporting communities in need
* Sharing God's love through practical service

## Our Churches

Faithful Labourers works with churches and fellowships in different communities:

* **Kawempe Pentecostal Church** — Kampala
* **Kisimu Pentecostal Church** — Wakiso
* **Kikoza Pentecostal Church** — Luwero


The website allows visitors to:

* Learn about Faithful Labourers
* Learn about our churches
* View ministry activities and photos
* Learn about our outreach programs
* Contact us
* Find our contact information
